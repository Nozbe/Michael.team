## Project list - no sub-projects!
### Get organized in GROUPS and SECTIONS

![Projects - Groups - Sections center](/img/26-projects.jpg)

Now, Can you believe that Nozbe survived 19 years without adding sub-projects? That's right. We always believed in simple flat project structure.

We know that a structure of projects, sub-projects, then tasks and sub-tasks may appear to look better. But it's not sustainable! 

Over the years we had many customers leave us for other systems because we didn't offer sub-projects - to them - an obvious feature - but later many would come back saying they found our simple structure helped them get things done better!

Without sub-projects in Nozbe you can still get organized pretty well:

First off, you can group projects together. It's better than sub-projects or project folders because one project can belong to several groups. Like this "nozbe.com web site" project can belong to both marketing and development groups.

Second - you can add sections to a project - to make them more scrollable. They say scrolling is like falling - so in new Nozbe you can divide a project into different groups of tasks - sections - you can hide them, move them up or down and of course move tasks between sections. Many of our customers use it as a vertical Kanban view.

## Task Comments 💬

### Notes with video, paperless office and checklists

Task comments in Nozbe look simple but they are powerful! When back in the day we introduced comments to tasks in Nozbe Classic, we realized this was a game changer because they can serve many purposes.

![Add comment - View comment - Player center](/img/26-comments.jpg)

Use Comments as Notes! Even if you work alone, add a comment to a task to better describe a task for yourself, especially when a task repeats once a year, you might not remember what needs to be done - the future you will thank you for detailed instructions.

I use comments to post progress on what I'm doing. As a way to journal - after each step I add a comment…

You can also embed YouTube or Vimeo videos for better instructions and view them inside the app!

Many of our customers are using Nozbe as their "paperless office". You can upload attachments to the task so you have all the documentation at hand.

And now on an iPhone you can directly scan a document to Nozbe

For example: at Nozbe we share a project with our accounting company. I create a task, scan the invoice, explain what's the reason for the cost in a comment and delegate to my accountant. Done and done.

As I mentioned, at Nozbe we want to keep a simple structure. No sub-projects or sub-tasks. We solved sub-tasks with checklists in comments.

In Nozbe Classic we introduced a type of comment that is a checklist. In new Nozbe we made checklists as a part of the comment. So you can have one checklist as a comment, or you can have several smaller checklists.

But remember, if a checklist has grown too much, you can still convert it to a project with one click!

## Task-based communication!

![Comments - Links - Pinned center](/img/26-pinc.jpg)

Now comes the kicker - at Nozbe we NEVER email each other.

Let me repeat: we don't send emails to each other in our team.

We communicate through tasks.

This makes our communication focused. We discuss everything related to a task in the comments. And if during a discussion we go off topic, we create a new task for it. We exchange comments to work together better.

In Nozbe Classic when we introduced comments, the latest comment was at the top of the list… however as we started really working a lot more together and our company grew, we realized, this is not how reading works.

When you read a message thread - you want to be able to read from top to bottom. This is natural.

Reading from bottom to top is not - it requires moving up, reading down, moving up, reading down… and if you have several comments to catch up on, it's really annoying.

That's why in new Nozbe it's the same way as in your favorite chat program. Like in WhatsApp or Messages app. You read from top to bottom.

That's why we say - Nozbe is like WhatsApp but a lot more organized! In fact, many customers come to us because they tried to manage their company via WhatsApp and started failing.

You can also mention people - they'll be notified that you talked about them in this task. 

Sometimes the shortest comment is just a reaction. You can add one of 5 reactions to a comment. We love it especially if you're asking someone with a mention and are waiting for their confirmation and when you get it in a form of "thumbs up" it's so satisfying.

You can also easily link to other projects or tasks… When a task linked to a comment is done, you'll see its state changes.

And lastly - when a task has too many comments, how to distill signal from noise? Simply pin the comments that are really important or describe the task best. Later you can view all the pinned comments to catch up easily.

## Collaboration in projects

![Project - Limited - Joint](/img/26-sharing.jpg)

Back in 2007 when I launched Nozbe, you couldn't share projects with anyone. Projects were yours only. Later we introduced "sharing" of projects which was another game changer. This gave birth to our concept of task-based communication.

In new Nozbe we took this to a whole new level. And as with everything - we simplified it.

When your team or company grows and you start embracing our task-based communication, micro managing who is invited to each project can be annoying.

Also, we learned over the years from our own company and our customers, that when the team has access to more information, people feel more trusted, more empowered and are making better and more informed decisions.

Remember this form for creating a project? It's still as simple - put the project name and hit ENTER. By default the project will be accessible to everyone on your team. No need for invites. You just create a project, add tasks, delegate them to others or mention people in the comments - this is how they find out about it.

Of course sometimes you don't want to share a project with everyone yet, so you can limit the project to just yourself or to only few people. While we have projects like this, in practice most of our projects are accessible to everyone.

And you can share a project with a different team - When I talked about scanning a document for my accountant I mentioned that we share a project with them. Just like in Nozbe Classic you could share a project with people from outside of your team, you can do the same with projects in the new Nozbe.

We call it "joint projects" as these are projects shared between two teams - on both sides you can decide how many people from each team participate in such a project.

## Priority view

### ⭐️ Manually "star" your next actions!

![Left Priority center](/img/26-priority.jpg)

To make a task a priority - something I want to work on - back in 2007 I basically took the design from Gmail where you could "star" an email message. In Nozbe you can star a task.

As I mentioned, good design lasts decades and this is still the best way to mark a task as something you want to work on. As your "next action" in GTD speak.

In Nozbe Classic we made automations, that triggered some tasks to become your priority automatically. Like when a date of the task was due, or like when someone delegated a task to you.

Again, just like with other things, when we started using Nozbe exclusively as our way of getting things done together - implementing task-based communication, we realized that it wasn't OK for people to add tasks to our priority list.

It's like when you have a priority list you prepared for the day and suddenly someone just slams a task in your face and puts it in front of you. Normally they'd put it in your inbox.

## Incoming - Notification Center

### 📥 Dates, Reminders, Mentions, Delegations…

![Right Incoming center](/img/26-incoming.jpg)

That's why in new Nozbe we've built "Incoming" view - which is our novel concept - list of tasks that require your attention:

- Tasks due today
- Tasks you're being reminded about
- Tasks delegated to you
- Tasks where people mentioned you
- Tasks added to Nozbe from other places like email integration that we just talked about

The goal of this view is to decide what to do next. You need to be reviewing all these tasks to clear out this view.

When you mark it as reviewed, it disappears from this view, but not from Nozbe.

When you perform an action on a task, like marking it with a star, or commenting on it, or delegating it further, the task is automatically reviewed.

Still, if you like to have tasks which are due for today automatically marked with a star and added to your priority list, you can set it up the Nozbe Classic way.
