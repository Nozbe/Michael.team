# No Office FM #98: Stop Using AI as an Assistant – Make It a Team Member Better Than You

Learn how to stop using AI just as a basic chat. Make it an autonomous team member that helps you manage and get your tasks done in Nozbe! In this episode, I sit down with my longtime friend and fellow CEO, Steve Parkinson of [Fydelia.com](https://fydelia.com), to discuss how small and mid-sized business leaders can integrate AI agents into their workflows without overwhelming their teams. From inviting an AI agent as a real space member in Nozbe via local MCP to managing 18 specialized bots across sales, coding, and email follow-ups, this conversation covers the practical, human, and strategic sides of working alongside AI.
