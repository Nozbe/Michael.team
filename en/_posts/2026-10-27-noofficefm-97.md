# No Office FM #97: How Michael Vibe-Coded an App Using AI and Nozbe

Ever wanted to build your own app without knowing how to code? See how [I vibe-coded a fully functional iOS app](/vibe) from scratch in just a few days using AI - and managed the entire process right inside [Nozbe][n]! In this episode, recorded live during Nozbe’s annual [company retreat](/reunion) in Gdynia, together with Magda I dive deep into the world of AI agents. Learn how I used Claude to build the Prezbe presentation app and connected local MCP to Nozbe for seamless, structured asynchronous collaboration.
