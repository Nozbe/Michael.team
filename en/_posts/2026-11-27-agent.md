## How to add a user to Nozbe that's an agent?

I've prepared a blueprint and a set of prompts for you to just copy and paste and your agent will know how to work in tasks with you - it'll post comments, mention you, delegate tasks back to you and be on the watch out for new tasks to do!

I've published it on GitHub: **[Agent Rules][a]**

So basically just follow these 7 steps:

1. **[Get a free Nozbe account](https://get.nozbe.com/agent)** as it's free with no time limit for up to 3 projects and 3 people (or agents) on the team with unlimited tasks, so you don't need to pay anything to start (but get a bonus if you use this link!)
2. Once you've got your account, **invite the AI agent to your team**. Go to "Members", hit "+" to "Invite new member to your space" and use another email address you can access, so you can confirm and set up your agent's account.
3. **[Get the Nozbe desktop app](https://nozbe.com/download/?c=michaelteam)** for Windows or Mac and install it.
3. **Log in to your agent's account** on the desktop app. Not yours. Your agent's! You stay logged in the browser.
4. Go to "Settings" → "Integrations" → "MCP" and **enable the local MCP connection**.
5. Open Claude Code/Codex/Antigravity in the folder (new or existing) where you want to work with the agent and **paste the MCP command into the chat**.
6. In that new chat, **paste the prompt from [setup.md](https://github.com/Nozbe/AgentRules/blob/main/setup.md)** to set up your agent. It'll automatically configure everything, including getting the [nozbe.md rules](https://github.com/Nozbe/AgentRules/blob/main/nozbe.md)
7. **That's it!** From now on, you can work with your agent directly through Nozbe: create tasks, delegate to it and discuss everything in comments. Good luck!
