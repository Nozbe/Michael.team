# No Office FM #96: How to Manage a Law Firm: From Chaos to Control

Did you know that most law firm owners work over 50 hours a week and constantly worry about court deadlines? Team communication is often scattered across different channels like WhatsApp, email, and texts. In this episode, we show you how to regain control over your deadlines and bring peace of mind to your law firm’s daily operations. Learn how to organize work in a law firm using a simple tool like [Nozbe][n] and streamline company operations and team communication without major overhauls - boosting your business efficiency along the way.
