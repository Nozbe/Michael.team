## Highlights from my "iPad Power User" profile…

Here are the main points [analyzed in the profile][l]:

- I wrote my University final thesis on working from home already… and 20+ years later I'm still working like this!
- I bought the first iPad because we were developing an iPhone/iPad app for [Nozbe][n] at the time…
- My current setup is an [M4 iPad Pro 13" 1TB Cellular](/ipadm4) with Magic Keyboard.
- I use [lots of peripherals with it](/backpack)
- In my [home office](/office) I have a [Mac Mini](/2tb) that's running my [recording studio](/studio)
- I started working on [Nozbe][n] because [I needed to get organized myself](/interview)
- [Nozbe][n]'s killer feature is [task based communication](/task)
- I co-authored [#iPadOnly book](https://ipadonly.com) with [Augusto Pinaud](/augusto) back in 2013 when the iPads were still mainly media-consumption devices
- I'm co-hosting several podcasts: [#NoOffice FM](/noofficefm) and [The Podcast FM](/podcast) in English and [NieMaBiura](/pl/niemabiura) and [Nadgryzieni](/pl/nadgryzieni) in Polish
- At [Nozbe][n] we are 15 at the moment and [we all work from our homes](/nooffice) and meet [once a year on a company reunion](/reunion/)
- I create lots of [Shortcuts](/shortcuts) and many of them are still [unpublished](/cuts)

These are just a few gems, I do encourage you to read the **[entire profile which has been very well put together by Mike and Matt at iPadProductivity.com][l]**
